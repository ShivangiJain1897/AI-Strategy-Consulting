Two ready-to-use PRD templates: a thorough one for a net-new product, and a lightweight one for a single feature. View them here, copy them, or download the editable Markdown.

Two types of PRD

Pick the right depth for the job

A product PRD sets direction for something net-new and covers the business case, architecture, and AI details. A feature PRD is short and fast — just enough to build one well-scoped change.
